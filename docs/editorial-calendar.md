## Measurement after activation

The current local tracking contract includes `page_view`, `project_view`, `service_view`, `campaign_view`, `brief_click`, `booking_calendar_click`, `brief_start`, `brief_success`, `brief_failure` and `email_click`. These events are plumbing; no reporting provider or visitor baseline has been selected/verified.

Once Cameron chooses reporting and privacy/consent handling, review monthly: article views, next-step clicks, brief starts, confirmed recorded briefs, failures and qualified enquiries by known service/campaign. A calendar click is not a booking; a received inquiry is not a client. Reconcile actual bookings and qualifications through an approved source before reporting them. Keep names, brief text, email addresses, arbitrary query parameters and click IDs out of general analytics.

Use the first measured month as a baseline. Decide the next brief from actual buyer questions and enquiry quality, then separately research any search opportunity. Do not publish traffic or conversion estimates as proven results.
